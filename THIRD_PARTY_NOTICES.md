Runtime components include Kotlin (Apache-2.0), AndroidX/Compose/Room/WorkManager
(Apache-2.0), Dagger/Hilt (Apache-2.0), kotlinx.coroutines (Apache-2.0), and
SQLCipher Android and its native cryptographic dependencies. Their original
copyright and license notices apply; upstream notices are retained in dependency
artifacts. SQLCipher licensing: https://www.zetetic.net/sqlcipher/license/ .

Development-only tools include Gradle (Apache-2.0), JUnit (EPL-2.0), and Robolectric
(MIT). No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

The planned PK implementation references TransmtfTeam/Transmtf-HRT-Tracker,
commit 8c9abdde, under MIT: https://github.com/TransmtfTeam/Transmtf-HRT-Tracker .
No upstream PK code is included in M1. Original MIT copyright and license text
must accompany the M4 port and its About-page attribution. Vico 3.3.1
(Apache-2.0) is reserved for chart milestones and is not shipped in M1.
