# France ANSM RCP 5.2 — E2/T formulation coverage and existing-model review (2026-10-30)

**Status: SOURCE AUDIT ONLY.** This is newly retrieved French official product-label evidence, not completed PK modelling, independent clinical validation, or permission to change a live estimate. No formula, parameter, production estimator, database, or APK was changed in this phase.

## Scope and method

- Primary authority: ANSM/Ministry Base de Données Publique des Médicaments (BDPM), product-specific **Résumé des Caractéristiques du Produit (RCP), §5.2 Propriétés pharmacocinétiques**, accessed 2026-10-30. Official product documents are regulatory summaries of underlying studies, not a complete machine-readable human concentration-time dataset.
- Reference source: https://base-donnees-publique.medicaments.gouv.fr/
- Repo comparison: `pk-engine/src/main/kotlin/net/plainnotes/app/pk/Engine.kt`, `Types.kt`, `pk-engine/src/main/resources/pk-params.json`, `app/src/main/java/net/plainnotes/app/conc/ConcentrationCalculator.kt`, `ui/E2Total.kt`, `ui/MedicationEditor.kt`. Checked on the default branch in this phase.
- User instruction: complete missing estradiol/testosterone formulation coverage and review **existing** curves against French official evidence; not just fill empty entries.
- Scope of a curve is **drug concentration** (PK), not suppression of endogenous testosterone or pharmacodynamic antiandrogen effect. A total serum testosterone measurement includes baseline/endogenous testosterone. An ester mass is not the mass of released parent hormone.
- Keep recorded historical dose, ingredient/ester, product, route, site and lab analyte metadata distinct. Never infer route, formulation, reference baseline or assay from a brand name alone.

### Explicit missing or limited coverage

- **E2 esters**: current editor offers EV/EC/EB/EN/EU for injection but the production engine only implements EV IM; no French ANSM RCP with enough comparable injectable E2 PK was verified for EC/EB/EN/EU in this audit. Do not claim universal lack of data worldwide or invent a depot half-life. Existing EV IM uses literature, not a French injectable product-specific RCP.
- **E2 SL EV**: French EV oral RCP is not SL-EV evidence; remain unsupported without route-matched human observations.
- **T cypionate/propionate/mixed esters; SC injections; T implants; oral TU; T patches**: adequate current French product RCP-specific PK was **not verified in this search**, not proven impossible. Historical Pantestone oral TU and Intrinsa T patch exist; historical approval/clinical use does not establish current availability or model suitability. Expand to other national regulators and EMA if no French source can be verified; record country and exact formulation.
- Distinguish *marketing authorisation*, *marketing/discontinuation status*, and *availability at a pharmacy*. No official RCP permits predicting testosterone suppression from CPA/SPI or conversion of T to E2 quantitatively by merely summing drug curves.

## Current code-to-evidence gap

- `Engine.Curve` has E2/CPA/SPIRONOLACTONE/CANRENONE/PROGESTERONE, but no testosterone curve. `MedicationEditor.MOLECULES` allows T recording. `ConcentrationCalculator.OTHER_MOLECULES` covers only CPA/SPI/P4, so T records don't enter PK simulation.
- `Engine.choose`: E2 oral E2/EV, sublingual E2 only, gel E2, patch E2, IM EV only; other E2 esters not modelled.
- `E2Total`: M2 SL + compatible non-SL E2 sums total display, with explicit caveats; do not change current mixture semantics blindly.
- The current parameter JSON's top-level `status` still claims models are not used by engine; this metadata is stale (runtime does reference `PkParams.models`) and should be fixed in a separately verified metadata-only change.
- New French RCP evidence must not silently overwrite previous published-literature evidence; preserve scientific source registry and old frozen lab/export snapshots.

Priority Estreva own model dispatch; oral E2, oral EV, Oestrodose, patch and oral P4 checks. For each candidate compare pre/post single+repeated dose and residuals; do not change production before explicit acceptance.

4. **T gels**: separate brand/vehicle-specific parameter tables (Androgel 1%, 1.62%, Testavan, Fortigel) because dose/time/site effects differ, and tests must not fit a monotonic curve to dose-titrated independent patient groups. No invented site factors.
