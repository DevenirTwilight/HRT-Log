## Interpretation and next implementation decisions

1. Official labels are original regulatory observations at population level. **Neither official-label fit residual nor an internal Kotlin regression is an independent validation cohort.**
2. Serum total concentration can include endogenous/background analyte. Direct model-vs-label comparisons above deliberately do **not** invent a background, and no quoted discrepancy is an estimate of clinical accuracy.
3. UTROGESTAN demonstrates a pronounced possible late-tail discrepancy. Next step should evaluate alternative single/two-phase P4 shapes with fitted-source versus independent-source separation and observed assay methods; only promote to production after documenting losses on the FDA Prometrium benchmark and acceptable uncertainty.
4. DUOVA comparisons are exploratory: oral EV co-formulated with MPA in postmenopausal women. Keep product identity, analyte and mass unit explicit; do not average `E1` or `E1S` into `E2`.
5. For `T`, French ANDROTARDYL and NEBIDO RCP report total testosterone, but not individual pretreatment baseline in the summarised data. Their 4.5-day and 90±40-day values concern depot release. Absolute user-level T injection curves require a documented baseline approach and model checking; cannot claim testosterone clinical accuracy by forcing total Cmax into exogenous incremental concentration.
6. This report is reproducible from the current machine-readable `pk-params.json`, the equations stated above, and official RCP observations. Output rounding is 2 decimals; comparisons need true model-vs-label numerical tests in later implementation.
