## Mathematics

For each candidate `c`, let the fast absorption input be a `n`-stage Erlang distribution with stage rate `k_f`; the slow input is first order with rate `k_s`. Both are convolved with a central first-order elimination `k_e`.

```text
h_c(t) = (1-w) * h_fast,Erlang(n,kf,ke,t) + w * h_slow,Bateman(ks,ke,t)
g_c(t) = h_c(t) / h_c(1 hour)
research_score_c(t) = SUM_over_real_recorded_doses( dose_mg * g_c(t - dose_time) )
                       only for t >= dose_time
```

`g_c` is dimensionless; `research_score` is **mg-weighted relative exposure shape**, not an estradiol serum concentration, patient-specific PK parameter, or drug-equivalence factor. No q6h/q12h/night-dose schedules are generated by default. Input dose events must reflect caller-supplied *actual* times.

An optional `ExperimentalStudyAnchor` can reconstruct a **named, explicitly hypothetical study scenario**:

```text
study_concentration(t) = background + amplitude_in_study_units_per_1mg_at_1h * research_score_c(t)
```

The anchor is not a cross-study or individual conversion factor. The model does not identify absolute bioavailability, distribution volume, body-weight dependence, dose linearity, assay bias, individual residuals, or clinical coverage. Repeated-dose **linear superposition is an assumption**, not a demonstrated universal property of sublingual estradiol.

## Usage (for tests / research console only)

```kotlin
import net.plainnotes.app.pk.experimental.*

val doses = listOf(
    ExperimentalDose(atHour = 0.0, mg = 0.5),
    ExperimentalDose(atHour = 5.0, mg = 0.5),
)
val candidate = ResearchSublingualV01.candidates.last()
val oneCurve = ResearchSublingualV01.relativeHistory(6.5, doses, candidate)
val modelSpread = ResearchSublingualV01.spread(6.5, doses)
// oneCurve and min/median/max are NOT pg/mL or a validated clinical interval.
```

Selection is **not** random sampling from a patient population. Min/median/max simply describe variation across 15 hand-filtered, already-exposed candidate fits; they are not confidence, prediction or credible intervals. In particular the long tail and repeated-dose trough may differ substantially across candidate assumptions.

## Important unresolved evidence

1. Price 1997 reported AUC0-24 per 1 mg SL of 2109 ± 1031 pg·h/mL, while manual digitization of Figure 1 does not reproduce the same value under the evaluated baseline assumptions. Different numeric estimands and the original calculation require further review.
2. Price Figure 1 is a **group curve** and digitization, not individual data. The same six crossover participants contribute the different Price doses, so those doses are **not separate cohorts**.
3. Rosano 1997 baseline for its PK25 cohort and within-person covariance remain unknown; Komesaroff dispersions are SEM rather than SD.
4. Yaish 2023 four doses per day does not prove regular q6h administration; dose clock, group-level mean-vs-median, covariance and immune-assay comparability prevent direct calibration.
5. No new independent fully timed, held-out pure-sublingual individual series is obtained here; the P2-R `G0_EXPLORATORY_ONLY` research state remains unchanged.
6. `transitStages` is a mathematical input order, **not** an anatomical compartment count.
