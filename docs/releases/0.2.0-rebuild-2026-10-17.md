<summary>English</summary>

**This is 0.2.0 rebuilt from the current default branch.** The displayed version stays 0.2.0; the Android build number goes from 5 to 29. Source commit `76627bf`. The previous build 5 (source `462da22`) has been removed from this page; its hashes are archived in the repository under `docs/releases/`.

Main features:

- Fully offline: no INTERNET permission, no account, analytics or ads. The external link on the About screen only opens your browser when you tap it.
- Medication schedules and reminders, stock and refill reminders; dose records, history and recycle bin.
- Estradiol (E2) concentration curves estimated from published studies, optionally calibrated with lab values you enter. They are conditional estimates, not measurements of your own blood levels; they cannot replace lab tests and give no dosing advice.
- Lab records, wellbeing (symptoms and how you feel) records, appointments and question lists.
- Treatment stage timeline and milestones.
- PDF report and visit pack export, CSV export. **Exports are not encrypted**; you choose where they are saved in Android's file picker.
- App lock (PIN, optional biometrics); records are stored in a SQLCipher-encrypted database.
- Encrypted backups (`.pnbak`, Argon2id + AES-256-GCM, password chosen by you; a forgotten password cannot be recovered).
- Calculator / Notes disguise (`full` flavor). Text typed into the Notes disguise screen is not encrypted. Disguise does not protect against root, a compromised system or someone inspecting installed apps.
- Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and French.

**Updating:** same private production signing key (certificate SHA-256 in `SIGNING_CERTIFICATE.txt`). The old public build 5 and earlier private test builds (≤ 28) can be updated in place, keeping local data; export an encrypted backup first and do not uninstall or clear data. Debug-signed CI installations cannot be overwritten: export an encrypted backup and keep its password before switching and restoring. Uninstalling deletes local data.

Privacy notice: `PRIVACY.md` in the repository (also offline in the app, About → Privacy, in four languages). In-place update on a physical ARM device has not been tested (NOT_RUN). `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the asset checksums.
