The device OS/Keystore, authenticated SQLCipher access, root authority and mature cryptographic primitives form the trusted base. A compromised OS/rooted device and forensic erasure of every memory copy are not established protections. No name/date/medicine similarity can establish a clinical association. Every necessary version, recycle-bin member and typed relationship must be represented; unknown or missing members are refusal conditions, not optional skips.
